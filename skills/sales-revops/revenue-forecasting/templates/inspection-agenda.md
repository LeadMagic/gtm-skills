# Weekly Forecast Inspection Agenda

## Snapshot

- Forecast period, data cutoff, and owner
- Prior forecast versus current downside, base, upside, and commit
- Closed revenue and remaining gap

## Movement Review

1. Category changes and the new buyer evidence behind each.
2. Slipped close dates and the event that failed to occur.
3. Amount increases or decreases and source of the change.
4. New material opportunities and why they were absent before.
5. Removed opportunities and correct closed-lost or nurture handling.

## Risk Review

- Deal and account concentration
- Stage aging and missing next events
- Economic buyer, champion, procurement, legal, and implementation risk
- Renewal, expansion, churn, or consumption assumptions
- Historical model changes and sample limitations

## Decisions and Change Log

Record category, amount, timing, assumption, owner, and next review date for every material change. Preserve disagreement rather than averaging incompatible forecasts.
