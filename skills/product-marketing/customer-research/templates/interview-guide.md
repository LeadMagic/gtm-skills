# Buyer Switch Interview Guide

## Setup and Consent

- Confirm the decision or event being reconstructed.
- Explain recording, storage, and quote-use policy; record consent.
- State that the conversation is research, not a sales or support call.

## Timeline Prompts

1. Take me back to the first time you thought the old way might no longer work. What happened?
2. What did you do next, even if it was informal or delayed?
3. What changed that moved you from passive concern to active search?
4. Which alternatives did you seriously consider, including doing nothing or building internally?
5. Who entered the decision, what did each person need, and where did the group disagree?
6. What evidence or event made the choice feel safe enough?
7. What nearly stopped the decision?
8. What happened immediately after purchase, and how did that compare with expectations?

## Four Forces Probes

- Push: What made the existing situation unacceptable?
- Pull: What progress did the new approach promise?
- Anxiety: What risks or unknowns made switching difficult?
- Habit: What made the current approach comfortable or easy to keep?

## Evidence Close

- Ask permission to follow up on contradictions or missing dates.
- Confirm whether quotes may be attributed, anonymized, or not reused.
- Record artifacts mentioned: evaluation sheet, internal memo, workflow, screenshot, or competitor comparison.
