## Evidence Hierarchy

1. Recorded or contemporaneously documented behavior from an eligible participant.
2. Direct quote with cohort, date, context, and permission metadata.
3. Repeated pattern supported by multiple independent interviews.
4. Researcher inference, visibly labeled and linked to supporting evidence.
5. Internal belief or hypothesis, never relabeled as a customer finding.

## Interview Boundaries

- Obtain consent before recording and follow the user's privacy and retention rules.
- Ask about a specific recent decision rather than general preferences.
- Do not expose one participant's confidential information to another cohort.
- Do not report a frequency or market percentage from qualitative interviews alone.
- Preserve disagreements and negative cases instead of forcing a single narrative.
