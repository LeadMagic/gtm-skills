# Neutral Win-Loss Interview Guide

## Opening and Consent

- Confirm the decision being discussed and the participant's role.
- Explain recording, retention, and quote-use policy; record consent.
- State that this is research, not a sales or account-recovery conversation.

## Timeline

1. What changed that caused the team to consider a different approach?
2. Which alternatives were seriously considered, including keeping the status quo?
3. Who participated, and what did each person need to believe?
4. Which evaluation criteria mattered at the beginning, and which mattered at the end?
5. What information, interaction, or event changed the direction of the decision?
6. What created confidence? What created concern?
7. How did implementation effort, risk, timing, and commercial terms affect the choice?
8. What was the final tradeoff the team accepted?
9. Looking back, what did the selling teams misunderstand about the decision?

## Evidence Close

- Confirm exact terminology and dates where possible.
- Ask permission for attributed, anonymous, or internal-only quote use.
- Note any evaluation artifact the participant is willing and permitted to share.
- Record follow-up questions without using the call to reopen the sale.
