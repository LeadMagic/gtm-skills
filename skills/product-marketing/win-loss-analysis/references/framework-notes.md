## Evidence Rules

1. Preserve buyer statements, CRM claims, seller interpretation, and researcher inference as separate fields.
2. Use recent decisions so participants can reconstruct events and stakeholders.
3. Code positive, negative, mixed, and absent evidence; silence is not a negative score.
4. Report sample counts and segment boundaries next to every material pattern.
5. Require corroboration before promoting a finding into a durable positioning or process change.
6. Route supported findings into an artifact and validation metric, not an unowned recommendation.

## Program Boundaries

- Do not turn interviews into sales recovery calls.
- Do not expose another buyer's confidential details.
- Do not publish attributable quotes without permission.
- Do not claim statistical prevalence from a qualitative sample.
