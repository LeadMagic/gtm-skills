# Public content policy

Publish only content intended for public distribution. Use fictional identities, reserved example domains, and nonfunctional credential placeholders in samples. Do not publish customer records, personal contact details, tokens, private logs, internal infrastructure inventories, contracts, or private financial information.

Preserve license and copyright notices. Link to third-party sources and use original summaries; do not reproduce restricted material without rights. References to products or people do not imply endorsement or affiliation. LeadMagic publishes these repositories; vendor examples are not independent rankings.

Do not invent security certification, universal compliance, delivery guarantees, customer results, testimonials, or comparative performance. Verify factual claims against current evidence and obtain permission for identifiable case studies. Hypothetical figures must be labeled as examples. See [FTC guidance on substantiation](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation).

Respect consent, opt-outs, platform terms, and applicable data-use requirements. Public availability of data does not by itself authorize every use. Legal and regulatory conclusions require appropriate review; this repository is not a compliance certification.

If a real credential was exposed, revoke or rotate it with the issuing service. Current-file cleanup does not erase Git history, forks, downloaded releases, or third-party caches. Report suspected exposures privately to [security@leadmagic.io](mailto:security@leadmagic.io).
